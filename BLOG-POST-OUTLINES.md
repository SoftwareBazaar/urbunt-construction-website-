#### 4. Common Construction Scams in Kenya (500 words)

**H3: The "Disappearing Contractor" Scam**
- Takes large upfront payment
- Disappears or delays indefinitely
- How to spot: Pressure for immediate full payment
- Protection: Milestone-based payments

**H3: The "Unlicensed Professional" Scam**
- Claims to be registered architect/engineer
- Provides fake credentials
- Results: Plans rejected, poor quality work
- Protection: Verify NCA registration online

**H3: The "Material Substitution" Scam**
- Quotes quality materials
- Uses inferior/fake materials
- How to spot: Below-market pricing, vague specifications
- Protection: Specify brands in contract, conduct inspections

**H3: The "Fake Invoice" Scam**
- Inflates material costs
- Pockets the difference
- How to spot: Refuses to show receipts
- Protection: Require original receipts, market price verification

**H3: The "Change Order" Scam**
- Lowballs initial quote
- Constant "unexpected" cost additions
- How to spot: Initial quote seems too good to be true
- Protection: Fixed-price contracts, detailed BOQ

**H3: The "Subcontractor Non-Payment" Scam**
- Takes your money meant for subcontractors
- Doesn't pay them
- Results: Work stoppage, liens on your property
- Protection: Pay some trades directly, verify payments

#### 5. Red Flags: Warning Signs of a Fraudulent Contractor (400 words)

Create a checklist with red flag emoji:
- 🚩 No physical office/business address
- 🚩 Asks for full payment upfront
- 🚩 No written contract or vague contract
- 🚩 Can't provide references or portfolio
- 🚩 Not registered with NCA
- 🚩 Pressure tactics ("deal expires today")
- 🚩 Cash-only payments, no receipts
- 🚩 Reluctant to put terms in writing
- 🚩 No insurance or bonding
- 🚩 Uses personal (not business) bank accounts
- 🚩 Constantly unavailable or changes numbers
- 🚩 Negative reviews online
- 🚩 Quote significantly lower than others (40%+ less)
