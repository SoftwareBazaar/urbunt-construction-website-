## Step 1: Add Watermark to Photos (2 Easy Options)

### Option A: Online Tool (Fastest - No Installation)

1. **Go to:** https://watermarkly.com/add-watermark/
2. **Upload** your 6 Naivasha photos
3. **Add Text Watermark:**
   - Text: `Urban T Construction`
   - Position: Bottom right corner
   - Font: Bold, size 40-50
   - Color: White (#FFFFFF)
   - Shadow: Black with 70% opacity
4. **Download** watermarked images
5. **Save as:**
   - `naivasha-exterior-1.jpg`
   - `naivasha-exterior-2.jpg`
   - `naivasha-roof-construction.jpg`
   - `naivasha-roof-timber.jpg`
   - `naivasha-bathroom.jpg`
   - `naivasha-chandelier.jpg`

### Option B: Use Canva (Free & Professional)

1. **Go to:** https://www.canva.com/
2. **Create design** → Custom size (same as your photo)
3. **Upload your photo** as background
4. **Add text:** "Urban T Construction"
5. **Style:** White text, black shadow effect
6. **Position:** Bottom right
7. **Download** as JPG (high quality)
8. Repeat for all 6 photos

## Step 2: Upload Photos to Admin Panel

1. **Login to admin:** https://urbantconstruction.com/admin
2. **Go to:** Media → Upload
3. **Create folder:** "Naivasha Project" (optional, for organization)
4. **Upload** all 6 watermarked photos
5. **Copy the URLs** of uploaded images (you'll need them for next step)
